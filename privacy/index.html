Kya Dekhein – Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026 · Developer: Sunil More

In short: Kya Dekhein has no account and no sign-in. It shows no ads and runs no analytics or tracking. It does not sell or share your data. Your profiles and likes stay on your TV.

What the app stores on your TV

To make picks for your household, the app saves these on the device only:

  • Profile names you choose (for example "Me" or "Kids") and whether a profile is a Kids profile
  • Your setup answers: languages, liked and blocked genres, and which streaming apps you use
  • Titles you mark as loved, okay, not for me or saved, and the last title you opened (for the "Did you watch it?" check-in)
  • A temporary cache of movie information and images, so the app loads faster

This data never leaves your TV and is not sent to the developer. You can delete a profile in the app. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes everything.

What the app sends to other services

To show titles, trailers and where to watch, the app sends requests to these services. The requests contain movie or series IDs and the text you search for. They never contain your profile names or likes.

  • The Movie Database (TMDB): titles, posters, ratings, cast and streaming availability. TMDB privacy policy
  • YouTube (Google): finding trailers and short reviews by title, and playing them with YouTube's player. Google privacy policy
  • Wikidata: matching a title to its page in streaming apps. Wikimedia privacy policy
  • Prime Video's public title pages: finding the link that opens a title directly in the Prime Video app
  • Streaming Availability API (Movie of the Night, via RapidAPI), when enabled: direct links to titles in streaming apps

Like any internet request, these services can see your TV's IP address and basic device information. Their own privacy policies cover how they use it.

Microphone and voice search

The app uses the microphone only when you press the mic button to search by voice. Your speech is turned into text by your TV's speech recognition service (on Google TV, a Google service), and the app receives only the text. The app does not record, store or send any audio itself.

Opening streaming apps

"Open in" launches apps such as Netflix, Prime Video, JioHotstar, ZEE5, SonyLIV, Apple TV or YouTube on your TV. Once you are in those apps, their own privacy policies apply.

Children

The app has a Kids profile that only shows family-friendly titles. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. All data stays on the device as described above.

Security

All requests to the services above use encrypted HTTPS connections.
